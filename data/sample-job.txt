Senior QA Automation Engineer
Halter — Auckland, New Zealand
Posted on Seek | www.seek.co.nz/job/78451209

About the role
Halter is looking for a Senior QA Automation Engineer to join our Platform team in Auckland.
You will own the automated test strategy for our mobile and web products and work closely with
developers, product managers and the release team.

What you will do
- Build and maintain end-to-end test suites in TypeScript using Playwright and Appium
- Design the mobile automation framework for our Android and iOS apps
- Work with developers to shift testing left and improve unit and integration coverage
- Run and monitor test pipelines in GitHub Actions, and triage failures quickly
- Support release readiness reviews before each production deployment

What we are looking for
- 5+ years of experience in software testing, with at least 3 in automation
- Strong TypeScript or JavaScript skills, and confidence reading application code
- Experience with Playwright, Cypress or similar web automation frameworks
- Hands-on experience testing native mobile applications with Appium
- Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines, Docker and AWS
- Good written communication and a habit of writing clear defect reports

What we offer
Competitive salary, employee share scheme, flexible hybrid working from our Auckland office,
and a hardware budget. Applications close 30 September.
