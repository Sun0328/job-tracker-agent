# Standing highlights

Background the introduction can draw on:

- Your working principle, in one line.
- How you came to this field: the career change, the degree, the move.
- Your current role and how long you have been in it.

Highlights the "Why I Am a Good Fit" section can draw on. Use only the ones the advert
actually asks for:

- A thing you have built, and what it does.
- A platform or integration you have worked with.
- A measurable improvement you made, stated plainly.
- Certifications and cloud experience worth naming.
