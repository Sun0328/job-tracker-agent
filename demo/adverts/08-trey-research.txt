Data and AI Engineer
Trey Research · Wellington · Fixed term, 12 months

Trey Research runs public-policy surveys and research for New Zealand government agencies.

The role
We are building a research assistant that summarises thousands of open-text survey answers and links each
finding back to the answers that support it. You will build the data pipelines and the LLM components.

You will
- Build data pipelines in Python and SQL (dbt is a plus)
- Use LLM APIs to classify and summarise survey text, with citations back to sources
- Build evaluation sets with our researchers and measure accuracy
- Handle sensitive data carefully and document every pipeline

You have
- 2+ years in data engineering or software engineering
- Strong Python and SQL
- Experience with LLM APIs or NLP
- Clear communication with non-technical researchers

12-month fixed term with a possible extension. Wellington office, flexible hours.
